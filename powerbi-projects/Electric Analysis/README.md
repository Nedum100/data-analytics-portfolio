⚡ Electric Vehicle Population Analysis Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes electric vehicle (EV) population data to understand adoption trends across different regions, vehicle types, and manufacturers. The dashboard provides insights into how electric vehicles are distributed by county, city, model year, and utility providers.

The analysis focuses on identifying growth patterns, popular EV models, and the spread of battery electric vehicles (BEVs) and plug-in hybrid electric vehicles (PHEVs).

❓ Problem Statement

As electric vehicle adoption increases, policymakers, utility companies, and transportation planners need clear insights into:

Which regions have the highest EV adoption

Trends in EV growth by year

Popular EV manufacturers and models

Distribution of BEVs vs PHEVs

The relationship between EV range, eligibility for clean fuel incentives, and location

Without structured analysis, it is difficult to track EV growth, plan charging infrastructure, and evaluate clean energy initiatives.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze EV population by county and city

Track adoption trends over different model years

Compare Battery Electric Vehicles (BEV) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEV)

Identify top manufacturers and models

Evaluate CAFV (Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle) eligibility patterns

Provide KPIs for EV adoption monitoring

🧠 Approach & Strategy Used
Data Preparation

The dataset was cleaned and transformed by:

Standardizing location and vehicle type fields

Handling missing or zero values in MSRP and electric range

Formatting geographic and categorical data for analysis

Analysis & Visualization

Power BI was used to:

Build interactive dashboards showing EV distribution across regions

Visualize year-over-year growth in EV adoption

Compare BEV vs PHEV populations

Display manufacturer and model popularity

Provide geographic insights using location data

This strategy was chosen because Power BI supports interactive filtering, geographic mapping, and clear presentation of large datasets.
