📊 HR Analytics Dashboard 
📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Human Resources (HR) data to gain insights into employee demographics, performance, and attrition trends within an organization. The dashboard helps HR teams and management understand workforce behavior, identify risk factors for employee turnover, and improve employee retention strategies.

The analysis focuses on employee attributes such as department, job role, age, income level, satisfaction, and years of experience.

❓ Problem Statement

Organizations often struggle to understand:

Why employees leave the company (attrition)

Which departments have the highest turnover

How employee satisfaction impacts performance and retention

The relationship between income, experience, and attrition

Without clear analytics, HR decisions are often made based on assumptions instead of data.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze employee attrition patterns

Identify departments and job roles with high turnover

Understand the impact of salary, age, and experience on attrition

Track workforce KPIs such as total employees, attrition rate, and average income

Provide insights for HR policy and retention improvement

🧠 Approach & Strategy Used
Data Preparation

The HR dataset was cleaned and structured by:

Handling missing and inconsistent values

Standardizing categorical fields (departments, roles, education levels)

Ensuring numerical fields were properly formatted for analysis

Analysis & Visualization

Power BI was used to:

Build interactive dashboards with filters for departments and roles

Visualize attrition trends across age groups, salary bands, and job levels

Track KPIs for quick HR performance monitoring

This approach was chosen because Power BI enables dynamic exploration of HR metrics and effective storytelling for business stakeholders.
