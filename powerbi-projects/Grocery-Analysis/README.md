📊 BlinkIT Grocery Sales Analysis Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes grocery sales data from BlinkIT to understand product performance, outlet efficiency, and customer purchasing patterns. The dashboard provides key business insights to help improve sales strategy, inventory planning, and outlet-level decision making.

The analysis focuses on sales trends across different product categories, outlet types, locations, and customer preferences such as fat content and product visibility.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze total sales across different product types

Compare performance by outlet size, type, and location tier

Identify top-selling and low-performing categories

Understand customer preferences based on item attributes

Provide KPIs for quick business decision making

🧠 Approach & Strategy Used
Data Preparation

The dataset was cleaned and structured to ensure:

Consistent fat content categories

Proper data types for sales, ratings, and years

Removal of inconsistencies for accurate analysis

Analysis & Visualization

Power BI was used to:

Create interactive dashboards

Visualize KPIs such as total sales, average rating, and product count

Compare outlet performance across different dimensions

Identify sales trends and patterns

This strategy was chosen because Power BI allows dynamic filtering, clear storytelling, and real-time business insights.
