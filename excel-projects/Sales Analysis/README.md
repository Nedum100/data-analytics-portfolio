📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard – Management Summary
Project Objective

This project was developed to transform raw sales data into a clear, reliable, and analysis-ready dashboard that supports informed business decision-making.

The dashboard is designed to answer key questions such as:

How are sales performing over time?

Which products, customers, and regions drive performance?

Where are growth opportunities and risks?

Problem Being Solved

The original sales data was fragmented across multiple tables and not structured for analytics. This made reporting slow, inconsistent, and difficult to interpret.

Without proper modeling:

Performance metrics could be inaccurate

Trends were hard to identify

Reports were difficult to scale and maintain

Strategy Used

A Star Schema data model was implemented, separating:

Sales transactions (Fact table) from

Descriptive business data (Dimension tables) such as product, customer, date, and territory.

This approach was chosen because it:

Improves report performance

Ensures consistent and accurate KPIs

Makes insights easier to understand and act on

Outcome

The resulting model provides:

Fast and reliable reporting

Clear visibility into sales trends and drivers

A scalable foundation for future analysis

This structure ensures the dashboard delivers trusted insights aligned with business objectives.
