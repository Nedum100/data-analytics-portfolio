🚲 Bike Sales Customer Analysis – Excel Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

This project uses Microsoft Excel to analyze customer demographic and lifestyle data to understand the key factors influencing bicycle purchase decisions. The Excel dashboard provides a clear and interactive summary of customer behavior across different income levels, regions, and demographic groups.

The analysis focuses on identifying trends and patterns that can help improve sales strategy and customer targeting.

❓ Problem Statement

Bike retailers need to understand:

Which customer segments are most likely to purchase bicycles

How income, age, and education impact purchasing decisions

The role of commute distance in bike demand

Regional differences in customer preferences

Without structured analysis, decisions are often based on assumptions rather than data.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze customer demographics and purchasing behavior

Identify high-performing customer segments

Understand the relationship between income and bike purchases

Evaluate commute distance influence on buying decisions

Compare sales patterns across regions

🧠 Approach & Strategy Used
Data Cleaning & Preparation

Excel was used to:

Standardize income values for calculations

Organize customer demographic data into structured tables

Remove inconsistencies for accurate analysis

Analysis & Dashboard Creation

Using Excel features such as:

Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts for summarization

Formulas for KPI calculations

Filters and slicers for interactive exploration

Excel was chosen because it allows fast data transformation, flexible analysis, and clear visualization without requiring additional tools.
